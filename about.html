favorite Our Story

We believe everyone deserves a taste of home.

Yum-Yum started with a simple idea — international students shouldn't have to choose between eating well and eating affordably. We connect home cooks with hungry students, one tiffin at a time.

Our Mission

Bridging the gap between home-cooked comfort and student life.

Moving to a new country is hard enough without having to survive on instant noodles. Yum-Yum gives local home cooks a platform to share their culinary traditions while giving students access to affordable, nutritious, and culturally familiar food.

Every tiffin delivered is a moment of connection — between a cook who cares and a student who misses home.
